A Century of Provenance

The Epochs of Mastery

Trace the century-long lineage of Time & Gold from our humble beginnings in Swiss atelier workshops to becoming India's pre-eminent salon for numbered complications.

1926 — Geneva, Switzerland

The Genesis of the Calibre Escapement

Master Horologist Émile Vaneau establishes our first restoration bench overlooking Lake Geneva, engineering custom hairsprings for royal pocket watches and marine chronometers.

1964 — Mumbai & Hyderabad

The Royal Indian Commission

Invited by the royal dynasties of the Deccan to curate bespoke high jewelry dials, setting rare Golconda diamonds and Ceylon unheated sapphires directly into Swiss automatic movements.

1998 — Vallée de Joux

Grand Complications Consortium

Formalized official direct partnerships with over 40 prestigious Swiss maisons, securing exclusive allocations for perpetual calendars, minute repeaters, and gyroscopic tourbillons.

2026 — Pan-India Flagships

The Sanctuary of High Horology

Over 65 luxury boutiques across Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, and Hyderabad offering bespoke private collector vaults, certified pressure testing laboratories, and GIA Type IIa solitaire atelier viewing.
