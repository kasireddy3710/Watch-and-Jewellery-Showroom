Horology Care

Complete Movement Overhaul & Calibration

Every complex caliber undergoes total disassembly, ultrasonic bath cleaning of all gear trains, re-lubrication using Swiss synthetic oils, and rigorous 360-hour chronometric testing in five thermal positions.

  • Complete disassembly & ultrasonic cleaning
  • Precision hairspring adjustment & timing
  • 2-Year International Service Warranty
Diagnostic Laboratory

Pressure Testing & Seal Replacement

Dry and wet vacuum pressure testing chambers simulate underwater depths up to 100 Bar. We replace all synthetic caseback o-rings, crystal gaskets, and triple-lock crown tubes to guarantee watertight integrity.

  • 100 Bar dry & wet pressure testing
  • Genuine Swiss o-ring & crystal gasket renewal
  • Same-day turnaround available at flagship salons
Aesthetic Restoration

Laser Micro-Welding & Case Polishing

Restore your timepiece to pristine factory condition. Our master polishers utilize high-precision laser micro-welding to fill deep scratches and dents on gold and titanium cases, followed by hand-satin brushing.

  • Advanced laser micro-welding for deep dents
  • Hand-finished satin brushing and mirror polishing
  • Preservation of original case geometry
